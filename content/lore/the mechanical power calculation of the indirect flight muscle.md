title: the mechanical power calculation of the indirect flight muscle

---
$$
P^*_{mech} = P^*_{ind} + P^*_{pro}
$$

mechanical power is the sum of the **induced power** and the **profile power**

**induced power** is the cost of lift
**profile power** is the cost of drag during flight

profile power proportional to the product of wingbeat frequency and amplitude cubed

there is a lot more math involved in these calculations. see Lehmann & Dickson 1997 for more specifics
