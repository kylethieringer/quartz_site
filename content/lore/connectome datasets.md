## fafb
full adult female brain
[zheng et al 2018](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2018.06.019)
## flywire
using the fafb dataset, all neurons were proofread and annotated through community effort
[dorkenwald et al 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2018.06.019)
[flywire](https://flywire.ai) - more info
[codex](https://codex.flywire.ai) - explore the dataset
## fanc
female adult nerve cord
[spelunker](https://spelunker.cave-explorer.org/#!middleauth+https://global.daf-apis.com/nglstate/api/v1/5727664734208000) - online visualization tool. need to be authorized to view
[wiki page](https://github.com/htem/FANC_auto_recon/wiki) 
[cave table dashboard](https://cave.fanc-fly.com/dash/)
[phelps et al 2021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2020.12.013) - dataset paper
[azevedo et al 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07389-x) - connectomic reconstruction
