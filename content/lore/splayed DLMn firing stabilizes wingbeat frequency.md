each MN spike causes an increase in calcium in the muscle fiber ([[DLMns adjust myoplasmic calcium levels to control wing power]]) and [[weak electrical coupling between DLMns favors splayed firing pattern]]. 

the splayed firing of MNs establishes a relatively stable calcium level in the muscle. This plot is showing the myoplasmic calcium levels produced by firing of each MN.
![[Hurkey.etal2023_image-7-x360-y552.png | 300]]

compared to the synchronized motor neurons firing: BIG fluctuations in the average calcium at the muscle. 

These can be visualized as readouts of the wingbeat frequency showing stable (splayed state) or big fluctuations (synchronized)
