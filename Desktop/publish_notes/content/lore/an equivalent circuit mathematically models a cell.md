an equivalent circuit represents all of the important electrical properties of a neuron using conductors, resistors, batteries, and capacitors. 

the lipid bilayer gives the cells [[capacitance]] but it is a leaky capacitor because of its ion channels that open and close

ion channels give membrane conductance but each channel must be considered separately.
