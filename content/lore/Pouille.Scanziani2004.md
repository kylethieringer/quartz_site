"==It is believed that action potentials convey information through their timing and frequency==”[Page ](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LTZZDXFI?page=&annotation=N53NM9UW) 
 
"==We therefore investigated whether distinct temporal features of a series of spikes in CA1 pyramidal cells—the principal hippocampal output—are selectively captured by local neuronal circuits==”[Page ](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LTZZDXFI?page=&annotation=5QTSXVUE) 
 
"==Recurrent inhibition was evoked by stimulating the axons of CA1 pyramidal cells at various frequencies with an electrode placed in the alveus==”[Page ](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LTZZDXFI?page=&annotation=BW9QMGQI) 
 
"==Specifically, alveus stimuli repeated at 0.2 Hz elicited recurrent inhibitory postsynaptic potentials (IPSPs) that were 25.3 ^ 1.8% smaller at the dendritic recording site than at the somatic one==”[Page ](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LTZZDXFI?page=&annotation=2I7PPM93) 
 
"==with series of two to four stimuli at 100 Hz==”[Page ](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LTZZDXFI?page=&annotation=YRRK7W3A) 
 
"==inhibition became progressively larger in the dendrites than in the soma==”[Page ](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LTZZDXFI?page=&annotation=5V23PR5N) 
 
"==These results indicate that recurrent IPSPs elicited at low stimulation frequencies are predominantly generated by perisomatic conductances==”[Page ](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LTZZDXFI?page=&annotation=Q2HNSHIT) 
 
"==Furthermore, the amplitude of IPSCs recorded in the V-clamped soma progressively decreased with increasing stimulus numbe==”[Page ](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LTZZDXFI?page=&annotation=33QYQS9P) 
 
"==after the onset of a series of stimuli delivered to the alveus, recurrent inhibitory conductances subside in the pyramidal cell layer and build up in more distal regions.==”[Page 2](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LTZZDXFI?page=2&annotation=PUVX58VD) 
 
"==stratum oriens that are therefore likely to receive recurrent excitation from CA1 pyramidal cells==”[Page 2](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LTZZDXFI?page=2&annotation=N3X7ECXN) 
 
"==the probability of spiking was highest at the onset of the series and rapidly fell with subsequent stimuli (‘onset-transient’ interneurons;==”[Page 2](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LTZZDXFI?page=2&annotation=4DISJYBK) 
 
"==second group (16 out of 49 cells) the probability of spike generation was lowest after the first stimulus and increased to a plateau between the third and tenth stimuli (‘late-persistent’ interneurons;==”[Page 2](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LTZZDXFI?page=2&annotation=XUKQDGDG) 
 
"==Spikes recorded in onset-transient interneurons showed very little variability in delay (‘jitter’; standard deviation, ^470ms; Fig. 3b), suggesting that they may operate as coincidence detectors==”[Page 2](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LTZZDXFI?page=2&annotation=Q59Y9PRI) 
 
"==strongly suggest that the onset of recurrent inhibition in the pyramidal cell layer and its subsequent shift to more distal regions result from the recruitment of distinct interneuron types==”[Page 3](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LTZZDXFI?page=3&annotation=FLIHEUNB) 
 
"==fast EPSC kinetics and membrane time constants may be essential in reducing temporal summation of  consecutive EPSPs in onset-transient interneurons1==”[Page 4](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LTZZDXFI?page=4&annotation=VRE48V39) 
 
"==slower EPSCs and membrane time constant observed in late-transient interneurons may prolong the time for effective summation of consecutive EPSPs==”[Page 4](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LTZZDXFI?page=4&annotation=4ABYQC3X) 
 
"==The disynaptic IPSP decreased  the window for temporal summation of consecutive EPSPs (Fig. 5a, b). This effect was more marked at the beginning than at the end of a series of stimuli==”[Page 5](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LTZZDXFI?page=5&annotation=8XHFQDNK) 
 
"==disynaptic IPSPs repolarize the membrane shortly after the first EPSP and are thus likely to contribute to the transience of the response in onset-transient interneurons.==”[Page 5](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LTZZDXFI?page=5&annotation=KMEZHHAU) 
 
"==The inhibition/excitation ratio was computed as the gabazine-sensitive charge divided by the gabazine-resistant charge and was found to be 2.1 times larger in onset-transient than in latepersistent interneurons==”[Page 5](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LTZZDXFI?page=5&annotation=5BXPH5AZ) 
 
"==inhibition contributes to temporally segregating the activation of early-transient versus late-persistent interneurons by CA1 pyramidal cells==”[Page 6](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LTZZDXFI?page=6&annotation=85PQ4WLZ) 
 
"==interplay between four properties, namely: EPSC kinetics, the membrane time constant, short-term plasticity and disynaptic inhibition of interneurons==”[Page 6](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LTZZDXFI?page=6&annotation=BHDKHVXE) 
 
"==coincidence detection and integration==”[Page 6](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LTZZDXFI?page=6&annotation=6XAETC5S) 
 
"==The coincidence-detection mode reports the onset of a spike series by activating perisomatic inhibitory conductances on pyramidal cells==”[Page 6](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LTZZDXFI?page=6&annotation=WYVCK7MW) 
 
"==We therefore expect the wide range of temporal activity patterns characteristic of CA1 pyramidal cells during exploratory behaviour in rats29 to be represented as a spatially distinct inhibitory pattern across hippocampal layers.==”[Page 6](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/LTZZDXFI?page=6&annotation=X9LY3TNH)
