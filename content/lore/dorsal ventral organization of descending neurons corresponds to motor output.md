title: dorsal ventral organization of descending neurons corresponds to motor output
publish: "true"
timestamp: 2025-04-10 17:47
---
wings are dorsally positioned. legs are ventrally positioned. 

the [[descending neurons]] associated with wings are found in a thin dorsal layer. 
the descending neurons associated with legs are in a larger ventral region.

However, despite the much different anatomical space discrepancy, the number of descending neurons is roughly equal.
