---
title: normative models predict behavior, but do not explain the mechanism
publish: "true"
timestamp: 2025-03-17 11:26
---
normative models can guide us in figuring out which variables might be important for a specific behavior (see [[normative models are concrete ways to predict animal behavior]]) but because it is a simple model it doesn't reveal which neurons are involved or responsible. 

there could be many different brains or circuits that all produce the same behavior with very different properties of the circuit. normative models cant distinguish between these
